If the Hormuz and sanctions situation stays foggy, and Trump's flat denial today keeps it foggy, that pressure doesn't ease.

Third, copper's quiet cousin: the AI-and-data-center build-out. As a citizen of the world parked in Chile, the thing genuinely worth tracking over the next months is whether the security-agenda negotiation and the fiscal squeeze pull in the same direction or against each other — that tension, more than any external shock, is what will shape the electoral year ahead.
