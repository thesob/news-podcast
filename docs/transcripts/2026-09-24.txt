In each case the confident public claim and the messy reality are arriving almost simultaneously. Authority is being issued and contradicted inside the same news cycle.

It's a near-miss, and near-misses are how systems learn — but it also crystallizes the argument that as machine output gets faster and more fluent, the scarce and valuable thing becomes human judgment applied at the right moment. The people who caught that error are worth more, not less, in an AI-saturated world. That's a hopeful counterweight to the anxiety, and it's showing up as a repeated theme.
