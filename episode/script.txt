[EN]

Hall (Stanford) and Michael Woodford (Columbia) "for microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation." The pair are towering figures in modern macro — Hall for his work on labour markets, recessions and consumption, Woodford for the theory underpinning how central banks set interest rates. The award, worth 11 million Swedish kronor, rewards decades of foundational theory rather than any single headline result.
