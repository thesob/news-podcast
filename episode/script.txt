That's the near-term worry for a copper-exposed fiscal budget like Chile's. The counterweight is that the very AI buildout people are nervous about is wildly copper-intensive, from data centers to grid upgrades to electrification, so the long arc could still favor copper even if AI equities get a haircut first. I'd hold both of those in mind at once rather than pick one.
